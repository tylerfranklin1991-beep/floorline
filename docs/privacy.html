Floorline Privacy Policy

Effective: October 23, 2026 · App: Floorline (com.tylerfranklin.floorline) · Developer: Tyler Franklin · Contact: feedback@floorline.app (placeholder; replace before publishing)

Floorline is a field floor-plan app. Your plans, photos, videos and notes stay on your phone. There is no Floorline account, and no Floorline server receives your data. We do not sell your data.

What the app uses and why

Permission / dataWhyWhere it goes
CameraRoom photos and videos, AR/camera measuring, photos of sketches for "Auto from sketch" and "Room shape from photos".Saved only in the app's storage on your device.
Photos / media you pickAttaching existing photos/videos, or sketch images to trace over.Copied into the app's storage on your device.
Location (precise/approximate), optionalTo geotag photos and the job with GPS coordinates when you ask it to.Saved only with that job on your device. It appears in exports only if you include it.
Local storageAutosaving your jobs, rooms, measurements and media for offline use.Your device's app storage. Uninstalling the app or deleting a job removes it.
Text recognition (on-device)Reading dimensions like 12'8 x 13'9 from a sketch photo.The image is processed on the device by Google ML Kit. It is not uploaded.

Data that leaves your device

  • Exports and sharing you start. When you share a PDF, PNG or JSON file, it goes to the app you choose (email, Drive, Messages…), under that app's privacy policy.
  • Feedback. "Send feedback" opens your own email app pre-filled with our address. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send there. If you attach a screenshot, only you choose which one.
  • Purchases (if Pro is offered). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We get a purchase confirmation and never see your card details. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Diagnostics from Google libraries. Google ML Kit (on-device text recognition) sends limited diagnostic data to Google to keep the feature working: device model, OS version, app version, performance metrics and a per-installation identifier. It does not send your images or text. Google Play services (in-app review and billing) work the same way. Floorline itself has no analytics, no ads and no trackers.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • We don't use advertising IDs or show ads.
  • We don't build profiles or track you across apps.

Children

Floorline is a professional tool and isn't directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can deny the camera or location permissions. The related features stop working, and everything else keeps working.
  • To delete your data, delete the job in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall. We hold no copy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
